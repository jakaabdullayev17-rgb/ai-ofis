# Asset sources

Runtime files are self-hosted with this repository; no external asset-service accounts or API keys are needed. Three.js remains 0.160.0 from cdn.jsdelivr.net.

## Poly Haven — CC0

The following assets are redistributed under CC0 1.0:
https://polyhaven.com/license
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

- `textures/wood_*.webp`: **Wood Floor**, Dimitrios Savva.
  Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_floor
  Original diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps resized to 1024px and converted to WebP.
- `textures/fabric_*.webp`: **Fabric Pattern 07**.
  Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/fabric_pattern_07
  OpenGL normal and roughness maps converted to WebP. Original colour pattern is not used; TEAM clothing colours remain.
- `environment/studio.hdr`: **Studio Small 09**, Sergej Majboroda.
  Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_09
  Original 1K Radiance HDR reduced in floating-point space to 512 × 256 for reflection use. Used for reflections, not as a visible background photograph.
- `models/lounge-chair.glb`: **Modern Arm Chair 01**, Vibrant Nordic.
  Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/modern_arm_chair_01
  Original 1K glTF repacked to one GLB, embedded maps reduced to 512px. Geometry retained.

## Original project model

- `models/office-head.glb`: original smooth character head made for AI Ofis.
  Rebuild with `blender -b --python assets/source/build_head.py`.
  No scan, real-person likeness, film/game character, or third-party model was used.
  This is an articulated-head visual replacement, not a full skinned humanoid rig or a claim of photorealism.

Car bodies and remaining furniture are still generated by `ofis.js`.
No real Mercedes/BMW CAD files have been imported.
